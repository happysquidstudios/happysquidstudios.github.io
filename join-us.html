Open Spot

Think you're a squid without the right tank?

We're a small team and we're not always hiring, but if you love games, don't take yourself too seriously, and reckon you'd fit in the tank, drop us a line.

What to send

  • Who you are and what you actually work on
  • A portfolio, reel, or a few things you're proud of
  • What you'd want to do here (art, code, audio, design, or surprise us)
  • Anything else worth mentioning

What happens next

  • One of us reads it, not a recruiter
  • No account managers, no six-week process
  • If it's a fit, we'll reply and grab a chat
  • If the timing's off, we'll hang onto it
