A wave of layoffs scattered a lot of talented people across the industry. A few of us stopped waiting for the next studio to give us a home, and built one in Middlesbrough instead.

We're a small team making our first original game. No publisher, no crunch, just people who love making games.

And the name? Martin has always called his two daughters his little squids. One day they were playing together and he said, "look how happy my little squids are." It stuck.
