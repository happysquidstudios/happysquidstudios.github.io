Who we are

Happy Squid Studios started the way a lot of good things start right now: a wave of layoffs scattered a lot of talented people across the industry, and a few of us decided to stop waiting for the next studio to build us a home, and build one ourselves instead.

We're a small core team backed by a wider pool of freelance friends, working together on our first original game. No publishers breathing down our necks, no crunch culture. Just a tank full of people who love making games.

Our squids have had their tentacles on bigger projects before making their own home:

Our First Game

Sweet Deceit

Get your friends together, open up shop, and keep the customers smiling. It’s chaotic, it’s co-op, and it’s sweeter than it looks, well right up until it isn’t.

Run your sweet shop, work together, keep the orders moving, and try not to ask too many questions about what happens behind the counter

  • Co-op chaos
  • Horror-comedy
  • 1–4 Players
