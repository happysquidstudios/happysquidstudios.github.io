Open Spot

Think you're a squid without the right tank?

We're a small studio and we're not always hiring — but we're always happy to hear from good people. If you love games, love making weird chaotic things with friends, and think you'd fit in the tank, say hello.

What to send

  • A little about you and what you work on
  • A portfolio, reel, or a few things you're proud of
  • What role you're hoping to fill (art, code, audio, design — surprise us)
  • Anything else you think we should know

What happens next

  • We actually read every message ourselves
  • No account managers, no six-week process
  • If there's a fit, we'll reply and set up a chat
  • If there isn't right now, we'll keep you in mind
